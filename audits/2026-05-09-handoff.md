## What v0.3.0 closes

All Critical, High, and 4 of 5 Medium findings from the 2026-05-09 internal audit are resolved on this branch. See `2026-05-09-fixes.md` for the per-finding commit map.

| Severity | In audit | Fixed | Documented-only |

| Critical | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| High | 6 | 6 | 0 |
| Medium | 5 | 4 | 1 (M-5) |
| Low | 5 | 3 | 2 (L-4, L-5) |

Notable architectural changes shipped:

- Mock family **removed** (audit C-4 / M-3).
- ECDSA and BLS standalone families **rewritten** to POA-shape with real cryptography (audit C-1, C-2, C-3, H-1).
- **Owner / Admin role split** on ECDSA + BLS service-managers (admin compromise can't reshape the operator set).
- **Snapshot quorum threshold** — historical envelopes evaluated against the threshold in force at their `reference_block` (audit M-4).
- **Strict snapshot reads** — missing snapshot rejects the signer; no "fall back to latest" path (audit H-2).
- **Checked Uint256 arithmetic** on weight transitions (audit H-3).
- **Duplicate `trigger_id` rejection** in every service-handler `save_envelope` (audit H-4).
- **`trigger_id` monotonicity bounded** by `MAX_TRIGGER_ID_GAP = 2^48` (audit H-5).
- **Two-step ownership transfer** on ECDSA + BLS (`TransferOwnership` → `AcceptOwnership`, `SetAdmin` → `AcceptAdmin`); Mirror uses single-step because the typical handoff target is a contract address that can't sign an accept (audit H-6).
- **Pause / Unpause** on ECDSA + BLS service-managers (owner-only; blocks weight-mutating writes and validation while paused).
- **Mirror post-deploy ownership handoff** — `stake-registry::TransferOwnership` and `service-manager::SetAdmin` exposed; CLI commands `registry transfer-ownership` and `service-manager set-mirror-admin` resolve C-5 at deploy time.
- **Trigger pusher allowlist** — `InstantiateMsg.allowed_pushers: Option<Vec<String>>` (audit M-1).
- **BLS DST locked** to `BLS_SIG_BLS12381G2_XMD:SHA-256_SSWU_RO_` (RFC 9380, byte-for-byte match with poa-middleware).

## Tests deferred to v0.3.x (auditor-relevant gaps)

Comprehensive multitest coverage of the new ECDSA / BLS standalone state shapes is the major outstanding gap before external audit. The deferred suites are scoped in `audits/2026-05-09-fix-plan.md` Phases B–E and tracked in `2026-05-09-fixes.md` "Deferred to v0.3.x follow-up":

- **ECDSA off-chain suite**: registration lifecycle, signing-key collision rejection, owner-only enforcement, pause lifecycle, validation boundary cases (single signer, multi-signer, exact threshold, off-by-one, zero signers, duplicates, unsorted, wrong message), snapshot regression at `reference_block`, two-step transfer.
- **BLS off-chain suite**: parametric mirrors of the ECDSA cases plus aggregate-signature validation, pubkey-collision rejection, zero-pubkey, wrong sig length / count, unsorted signers, snapshot regression.
- **Mirror gap-fillers**: explicit H-2 reproducer at the stake-registry level, M-4 quorum-threshold snapshot regression, and the C-5 deploy-handoff E2E (instantiate stake-registry, hand ownership to operator-sync-handler, push a `BatchSetOperatorDetails` envelope through the handler, assert it lands; analogous for service-manager `SetAdmin` → quorum-sync-handler).

A BLS test-signing helper (`bls12_381 = "0.8"` dev-dep with `Bls12381KeyMaterial`, `aggregate_g2`, `sign_g2` using the locked-in DST) is the precondition for the BLS suite.

## Known limitations

- `echo-with-id` and `simple-aggregator` wit-bindgen targets need `task components:fetch-wit SKIP_CLONE=true` (or the full `task clone-wit-repo` pipeline) before they build. CI populates this; local clippy excludes them via `cargo clippy --workspace --exclude echo-with-id --exclude simple-aggregator --all-targets --all-features`.
- The `#[serde(untagged)]` Wavs wrapper pattern is preserved per upstream `wavs-types` protocol convention; M-5 is addressed by C-3's auth gating + explicit JSON variant keys.
- Mirror handoff is intentionally **single-step** (no accept), because the target is a contract that can't sign an `accept` message. The canonical alternative — handler-deploys-downstream via `Instantiate2` — is functionally equivalent and deferred as a future architectural cycle.

## Recommended audit sequencing

1. Read `2026-05-09-internal-audit.md` for the original findings, then `2026-05-09-fixes.md` for the finding → commit map.
2. Walk the commit log on the `v0.3.0` branch (`git log --oneline`) — every audit finding is referenced in a commit subject.
3. Review the new ECDSA / BLS standalone surfaces under `packages/contracts/{ecdsa,bls}/`, focusing on:
   - `WavsValidate` signature paths (`is_valid_signature`, snapshot reads at `reference_block`).
   - Owner / Admin role split and Pause gating in execute handlers.
   - Two-step transfer state machine.
4. Walk the Mirror handoff surface: `stake-registry::TransferOwnership`, `service-manager::SetAdmin`, sync-handler `WavsHandle*` paths, and the CLI deploy commands in `packages/cli/src/main.rs`.
5. Re-check the digest contract is byte-for-byte consistent across `ecdsa/service-manager`, `bls/service-manager`, and `mirror/stake-registry`.
6. Cross-reference the locked-in BLS DST against RFC 9380 and `contracts/poa-middleware`.
